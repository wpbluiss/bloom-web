Couple-first

Made for both of you

Pregnancy happens to one body — and to two people. Bloom is the only companion designed for the couple, not just the calendar.

Partner accounts

Two phones, one journey. Your partner joins with a simple invite code and sees the same week, the same story, the same baby.

Shared check-ins

Gentle weekly prompts you both answer — how you’re feeling, what you noticed, what made you laugh. No pressure, no streaks.

For-you-both feed

A daily card for each of you — hers about the body, his about how to help — so you always have something to talk about at dinner.

Keepsake

A keepsake that writes itself

Nine months go by in a blur. Bloom quietly turns them into a book you’ll keep forever — one gentle moment at a time.

  • One-tap moments. A kick, a craving, a name idea — saved in seconds, before it slips away.
  • 37 hand-painted weekly illustrations. Every week of pregnancy, painted by a human artist — never generated.
  • A memory book that writes itself. Your check-ins and moments become a beautiful timeline — ready to look back on, or print.
  1. Week 8

    First ultrasound. A tiny flicker of a heartbeat. We both cried in the parking lot.

  2. Week 16

    First flutter. Like popcorn, she said. He kept his hand there all evening.

  3. Week 24

    Nursery day. Painted the walls sage green. Argued about the crib. Ordered pizza.

  4. Week 31

    The name. We said it out loud for the first time. It stuck.

Calm guidance

Answers without the anxiety

No doom-scrolling, no fear-mongering, no contradictory forum threads at 3 a.m. Just calm, human-written guidance for the question you actually have.

Food guidance

“Can I eat this?” — answered plainly, with the why behind it. Searched in two taps, explained in one sentence.

Daily cards

Written by humans

Every word in Bloom is written and reviewed by people — parents, editors, and clinicians. It reads like a letter, not a lecture.
